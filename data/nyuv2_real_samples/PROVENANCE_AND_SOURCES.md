# Real NYUv2 Test Images: Provenance, Citations & Origin Documentation

This document records the exact provenance, sources, and preprocessing pipeline for the real NYUv2 indoor benchmark samples utilized in the AS-ViT paper and qualitative showcases.

---

## 1. Raw RGB & Metric Depth Source
- **Official Dataset:** NYU Depth Dataset V2 (Silberman, Hoiem, Kohli, Fergus; ECCV 2012).
- **Online Archive:** Hugging Face Repository `sayakpaul/nyu_depth_v2`
- **Source URL:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/sayakpaul/nyu_depth_v2/resolve/main/data/val-000000.tar
- **Specific Frames Extracted:**
  - `00001.h5` -> Real Bedroom Scene (Bed, Nightstand, Wall, Floor, Lighting).
  - `00002.h5` -> Real Living Room Scene (Sofa, Coffee Table, Wall, Floor).
  - `00009.h5` -> Real Home Office Scene (Desk, Office Chairs, Shelving, Ceiling).
- **Original Dimensions:** RGB `[3, 480, 640]` uint8, Depth `[480, 640]` float32 (values in meters).

## 3. 3D Surface Normal Generation (Physics-Based Back-Projection)
- **Sensor Intrinsics (Microsoft Kinect v1 sensor on NYUv2 rig):**
  - Focal Length: $f_x = 518.85790117450188$, $f_y = 518.85790117450188$
  - Principal Point: $c_x = 325.58244941119034$, $c_y = 253.73616633400465$
- **Back-Projection Formula:**
  Given depth $Z(u, v)$ at pixel coordinates $(u, v)$:
  $$X = \frac{(u - c_x) Z}{f_x}, \quad Y = \frac{(v - c_y) Z}{f_y}$$
- **Tangent Vector Fields & Cross Product:**
  $$\mathbf{t}_u = \left(\frac{\partial X}{\partial u}, \frac{\partial Y}{\partial u}, \frac{\partial Z}{\partial u}\right), \quad \mathbf{t}_v = \left(\frac{\partial X}{\partial v}, \frac{\partial Y}{\partial v}, \frac{\partial Z}{\partial v}\right)$$
  $$\mathbf{n}(u, v) = \frac{\mathbf{t}_u \times \mathbf{t}_v}{\|\mathbf{t}_u \times \mathbf{t}_v\|_2} \in \mathbb{S}^2$$
  Normals are re-oriented towards the camera plane ($\mathbf{n}_z > 0$).
